## Search record and limitations

Searches included computational models of narrative, narrative economics, MovieGraphs, Narrative Networks, the exact phrase Narrative Systems Engineering, the Narrative Policy Framework, and narrative transportation. Queries were used to locate organizer, author, publisher, and program pages. Irrelevant search results were excluded.

Next review should use explicit inclusion criteria, search multiple scholarly indexes, follow citations, and compare measurement definitions and evaluations in full papers. Also examine narratology, semiotics, cultural analytics, opinion dynamics, and system identification. No claim of exhaustive novelty clearance is warranted.
