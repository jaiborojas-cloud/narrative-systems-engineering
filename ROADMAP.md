## Scope update from the founder's original idea

The initial scope is integrated audiovisual analysis. Before adopting PILOT-001, create the measurement inventory described in [the original idea](docs/ORIGINAL_IDEA.md), choose a few features across language and visual/material content, and draft one time-aligned audiovisual profile as a design example. PILOT-001 remains a candidate narrative subset. Stage 4 below is an optional extension; a validated audiovisual analysis framework can be a useful result without predicting audience responses.

## Stage 1: scope and research position

**Target:** weeks 1-2 after scope adoption. **Suggested lead:** founder.

Deliverables: a one-page problem statement, a comparison with at least 15 closely relevant works, working definitions, and a final decision on the provisional name. Search narratology, computational narrative, narrative reception, narrative policy, cultural analytics, and diffusion research. Follow citations in both directions and record inclusion criteria.

**Exit evidence:** one precise claim of added value, the closest alternatives, and an explicit explanation of how a proposed test could fail. If no difference survives comparison, reframe as a replication, benchmark, or application project.

## Stage 2: measurement pilot

**Target:** weeks 3-6, depending on annotator availability. **Suggested leads:** founder and a second independent annotator.

Deliverables: 6-10 short works with documented permitted uses, a codebook, development annotations, a frozen evaluation protocol, and an agreement report. Follow [PILOT-001](research/protocols/PILOT-001.md).

**Exit evidence:** labels usable under the protocol's provisional agreement thresholds, documented ambiguity, and measured annotation effort. If the codebook changes after evaluation, obtain a new untouched evaluation set.

## Stage 3: bounded computational baseline

**Target:** weeks 7-12; extend toward week 18 if capacity requires. **Suggested lead:** technical contributor, possibly the founder.

Deliverables: simple structured representation, reproducible extraction scripts, held-out evaluation, costs, and failure examples. Compare a simple text or rule baseline against the candidate approach. Avoid training a bespoke large model at this stage.

**Exit evidence:** performance by label and work, source-grounding audit, and a clear account of whether the approach adds value. A negative result can justify a revised measurement tool; it does not justify prediction claims.

## Stage 4: audience-response study

**Target:** months 4-6 or later, only after Stages 1-3. **Suggested leads:** founder with a researcher experienced in study design.

Deliverables: a named response variable, study protocol, sample-size justification, appropriate participant procedures, preregistered analysis, and comparison with simple baselines. Select one question such as prediction of event recall. Keep descriptive, predictive, and causal claims separate.

**Exit evidence:** independently measured human responses and an evaluation that prevents leakage across works and participants. Decide whether the result merits replication, revision, or stopping this hypothesis.

## Stage 5: replication and a research release

**Target:** months 6-12 or later. **Suggested leads:** founder and independent collaborators.

Deliverables: a second corpus or replication, concise paper, documented limitations, reproducibility package, confirmed contributor credits, and release terms. Prepare a public release only after the material is ready and publication is authorized.

**Exit evidence:** an independently checkable contribution. A DOI or repository is dissemination infrastructure, not evidence that the theory is true or uniquely original.

## Longer-term direction

Years 1-2: test transfer across genres, languages, and contexts; study one temporal process if data support it. Years 2-3: consider a sustained collaboration or lab when repeated results and funding justify it. Broader simulation and domain-specific engineering depend on those results.

The source visions extending to 2036 and 2050 remain historical ambitions. This roadmap does not assign confident dates to establishing a discipline, universal metrics, or social digital twins.
