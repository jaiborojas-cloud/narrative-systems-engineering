# Original idea: integrated analysis of audiovisual works

Recorded September 8, 2026, from the founder's clarification in this conversation. The English rendering below preserves the substance of the Spanish statement; it is not a verbatim quotation. No earlier date of conception is established here.

## Founder's original concept

The idea has been developing for some time: an integrated analysis of films, movies, and videos, beginning with an exploration of what can be measured and how.

At the linguistic level, possible subjects include screenplay complexity, intentions behind dialogue, word frequencies, semantic maps showing associations with concepts such as love, hatred, and desire, accents, and communicative intent.

At the visual and material level, the analysis could record landscapes, predominant colors in different contexts, clothing, objects, and recurring objects. Bringing these observations together through AI-assisted analysis could support a deeper and more quantifiable understanding of audiovisual works.

## Working research formulation

Develop and evaluate an integrated framework for measuring and interpreting linguistic, acoustic, visual, and material features of audiovisual works, with every result linked to its source evidence and measurement procedure.

This formulation is our proposed operational restatement. It does not claim that every feature is measurable with equal reliability, that interpretation is exhausted by measurement, or that audience effects can be inferred from the work alone.

## Relationship to the later documents

The conceptual sequence is now: original audiovisual inquiry -> HNA -> NDE -> NSE. The initial inquiry is broader than narrative-event extraction: it includes form, sound, visible environments, clothing, and objects. HNA expands it into an ambitious measurement system; NDE introduces human and social dynamics; NSE proposes a disciplinary umbrella.

The original inquiry can succeed without the predictive and societal extensions succeeding. Preserve the ambition to contribute a new area of study while evaluating the audiovisual framework on its own merits. NSE remains a provisional umbrella name; this clarification does not settle final naming.

## Proposed measurement inventory

These are candidate operationalizations for investigation, not validated project metrics.

| Dimension | Initial observable or annotation | Interpretation requiring additional care |
|---|---|---|
| Language | Word and phrase counts in a checked transcript; counts per 1,000 words | Frequency does not establish thematic importance |
| Script complexity | Separate sentence-length, vocabulary, and syntactic descriptors | No single descriptor measures overall artistic or narrative complexity |
| Semantics | Time-linked passages associated with a defined concept vocabulary | Negation, irony, speaker perspective, and implicit meanings affect labels |
| Dialogue intent | Annotated speech acts with supporting dialogue and context | Character intent and filmmaker intent are different; inferred intent remains an interpretation |
| Voice and accent | Speech rate, pauses, and qualified descriptions of pronunciation patterns | Accent labels need a defined rubric; do not infer identity from pronunciation |
| Landscapes and settings | Time intervals assigned to explicit setting categories | A depicted location does not necessarily identify the filming location |
| Color | Color distributions by shot or scene under a specified sampling procedure | Color-emotion or symbolic associations require separate interpretation |
| Clothing | Visible garment types, colors, and recurrence | Material, social status, or symbolic meaning may be uncertain |
| Objects | Presence, screen duration, and recurrence with stable identifiers | Repetition alone does not establish symbolic significance |
| Cross-modal relations | Co-occurrence of dialogue, sound, color, and entities in aligned intervals | Co-occurrence does not establish causal influence or audience response |

Each candidate should specify its unit of analysis, denominator, evidence source, method, missing-data handling, uncertainty, and validation procedure. For example, distinguish recurring shots of the same object from multiple objects of the same type.

## Proposed first artifact: an audiovisual measurement profile

Begin with one short work as a design example and a time-aligned table containing dialogue, language descriptors, concept annotations, acoustic observations, setting, color, clothing, and objects. Add an evidence-linked interpretation column that remains distinguishable from measurements.

Choose only a few features for the first implementation, such as word frequency, sampled color distribution, and annotated object recurrence. Keep the full inventory visible so that the pilot does not silently redefine the project as language-only or narrative-only research.

The one-work profile is for method development and identifying measurement problems. It is not evidence of general validity. Freeze a manageable subset afterward and evaluate it on additional works with independent annotation. The existing PILOT-001 addresses a narrative subset and remains optional rather than the sole entry point.

## Terminology note

The founder uses meta-analysis to describe combining many analytical layers. In research writing, **integrated multimodal analysis** or **cross-modal synthesis** describes that task more precisely. Reserve **meta-analysis** for a later statistical synthesis of results across studies. This terminology adjustment preserves the original intent.
