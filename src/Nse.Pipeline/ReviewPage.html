Visual annotation review

Record what is visible in each sampled frame. Keep labels specific and explain the visual evidence. A still image supports a visible pose, but cannot establish motion, intent, speech, or recurrence.

Annotation guide
  • Objects: visible non-garment items. Example: “rectangular display,” supported by “bright rectangular surface on the left.”
  • Clothing: visible garments and accessories. Use position to distinguish people; do not infer identity, profession, or status.
  • Setting: visible environment. Prefer “interior with a display” over a speculative location.
  • Actions: visible poses or interactions, such as “holding a rectangular item.” Do not infer a conversation or a movement from a still frame.
  • Not reviewed means no judgment yet. None visible means you inspected the frame and found no codable instance. Observed requires clear evidence. Uncertain requires an explanation of ambiguity and at least one uncertain observation.
  • “Clear” and “uncertain” are reviewer judgments, not calibrated probabilities. This is a design example, not validated ground truth.
