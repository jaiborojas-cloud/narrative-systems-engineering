Multimodal Narrative Systems Engineering: Signal-Grounded Metrics and State-Space Control Dynamics for Audiovisual Media

Jair Ivan Rojas Ruiz (Jair Rojas)

Abstract — Narratives across film, television, political discourse, and digital media are typically analyzed either through qualitative humanities frameworks or through isolated text-only natural language processing. Conversely, recent attempts to prompt large language models (LLMs) to score “narrative impact” or “emotional intensity” on arbitrary 0–100 scales suffer from uncalibrated units, stochastic hallucination, and complete detachment from the physical audiovisual signal. In this paper, we introduce Narrative Systems Engineering (NSE) and its computational core, Computational Narrative Dynamics (CND), a signal-grounded mathematical framework originated by Jair Ivan Rojas Ruiz that models audiovisual narratives as multimodal time-series inputs u(t) = (uac(t), uvis(t), uling(t)) acting upon a bounded cognitive-social state space x(t+1) = f(x(t), u(t)). We formally define and operationalize six core narrative quantities: (1) Narrative DNA as a signed, time-anchored entity-belief tripartite graph; (2) Narrative Entropy (H) in Shannon bits over chromatic and normative-stance distributions; (3) Narrative Energy (E) via a non-collapsing cross-modal resonance functional coupling vocal prosody (dBFS, VAD), visual flux (Jensen-Shannon divergence across color distributions), and lexical-normative cadence; (4) Narrative Complexity (C); (5) Narrative Inertia (I); and (6) Narrative Controllability via the discrete-time Controllability Gramian Wc. We validate the framework empirically across a three-work, bilingual (Spanish/English) benchmark corpus (EXP-001, EXP-002, and EXP-003, totaling 513.6 s and 77 time windows), demonstrating clean separation across civic debate, acute fear-appeal campaigning (LBJ's 1964 “Daisy” spot), and institutional crisis consolation (Reagan's 1986 Challenger address), while solving all 6! = 720 counterfactual montage permutations to achieve a 62.0% algorithmic reduction in terminal normative bias.
Keywords: Narrative Systems Engineering, Computational Narrative Dynamics, Multimodal Signal Processing, Shannon Narrative Entropy, Narrative Inertia, State-Space Control.

1. Introduction

Human societies are structured, stabilized, and transformed by the transmission of symbolic narratives across audiovisual and linguistic media [5, 6, 7]. However, bridging the gap between raw media signals (pixels, acoustic waveforms, lexical tokens) and higher-order cognitive and social dynamics remains an open scientific and engineering problem.

Existing computational approaches divide into two disconnected paradigms: (1) low-level media descriptors (shot boundaries, histograms, or transcripts) that do not model persuasive coupling [4], and (2) uncalibrated generative summaries that prompt LLMs for arbitrary scores. Defining Narrative Energy as a naive product E = Intensity × Coherence × Repetition × Identification fails dimensional analysis and collapses to zero whenever any single factor vanishes.

To establish Narrative Systems Engineering (NSE) as a falsifiable discipline [1], every metric in our engine (Argon.Engine v0.3) satisfies four strict criteria: (i) Signal Grounding in 16-bit PCM audio samples, 64×64 RGB24 rasters, and time-aligned speech tokens; (ii) Stated Units and Bounds (Shannon bits [2, 3], dBFS, and bounded state vectors in [−1, 1]K); (iii) Layer Separation between observable work material, symbolic interpretation, and state response; and (iv) Executable Reproducibility.

Terminology Disambiguation & Chronological Priority: While homonymous, purely qualitative or essayistic uses of the phrase “Narrative Systems Engineering” exist on the public web (including game-design notes or qualitative screenplay taxonomies such as Strangium's November 2, 2026 NSE-001 post), those texts contain no mathematical formulations, no physical audio/video signal processing, no state-space control models, and no software. In contrast, the work of Jair Ivan Rojas Ruiz—cryptographically archived since September 8, 2026 and registered in Zenodo under DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23987656 (ORCID: 0009-0003-1109-4583)—formally establishes the quantitative, signal-grounded discipline of Multimodal Narrative Systems Engineering & Computational Narrative Dynamics (NSE/CND).

2. Multimodal Signal Vector u(t)

Let an audiovisual work of duration T seconds be partitioned into N discrete time windows t ∈ {0, 1, ..., N−1} of width Δt. At each window t, the work emits a multimodal signal vector u(t) = (uac(t), uvis(t), uling(t)).

2.1 Acoustic Prosody Sub-Vector uac(t)

From the downmixed mono 16 kHz PCM16 waveform containing M normalized samples si ∈ [−1, 1] within window t, we compute Root-Mean-Square amplitude and decibels full scale:

RMS(t) = [ (1/M) Σ si2 ]1/2,    LdB(t) = 20 log10(max(RMS(t), 10−5)) (1)

Sub-framing window t into 100 ms intervals yields the Speech Activity Ratio VAD(t) ∈ [0, 1] and intra-window dynamic range ΔL90-10(t), normalized into acoustic intensity Iac(t) ∈ [0, 1]:

Iac(t) = clip(0.55 L̂dB(t) + 0.30 VAD(t) + 0.15 (ΔL90-10(t)/18), 0, 1) (2)

2.2 Visual Dynamics Sub-Vector uvis(t)

For each frame resized to a 64×64 RGB24 raster (4096 pixels), we compute ITU-R BT.709 luminance Yp, spatial contrast σY(t), HSV saturation, and the 512-bin quantized color distribution Pt(b). Camera cuts and visual flux are measured via the Jensen-Shannon Divergence in bits [3]:

DJS(Pt || Pt−1) = ½ DKL(Pt || M) + ½ DKL(Pt−1 || M) ∈ [0, 1] bits (3)

where M = ½(Pt + Pt−1). A hard scene cut is detected deterministically whenever DJS(t) ≥ 0.30 bits. Visual salience ψvis(t) couples DJS(t), contrast σY(t), and chromatic Shannon entropy Hvis(t).

3. Six Core NSE Metrics & Control

3.1 Narrative Energy E(t) & Cross-Modal Resonance

To prevent zero-collapse while rewarding vocal-lexical synchronization, Narrative Energy E(t) ∈ [0, 100] is formulated via the geometric Cross-Modal Resonance functional Φsync(t) = [Iac(t) · Iling(t)]1/2:

E(t) = 100 · clip(0.34 Iac(t) + 0.22 ψvis(t) + 0.29 Iling(t) + 0.15 Φsync(t), 0, 1) (4)

3.2 Shannon Narrative Stance Entropy Hnarr(t)

Across K = 4 belief dimensions (8 signed poles pk,±(t) weighted by cumulative energy exposure), Shannon Narrative Entropy [2] is:

Hnarr(t) = −Σk,s pk,s(t) log2 pk,s(t) ∈ [0, 3.00] bits (5)

3.3 Narrative Inertia I(t) & LTV State-Space Control

Narrative Inertia I(t) = clip(0.55 Ainst(t) + 0.25 ||x(t)||1/K + 0.20 (t+1)/N, 0, 1) damps state susceptibility in the discrete-time Linear Time-Varying (LTV) opinion state equation [4]:

xk(t+1) = (1 − γk(t)) xk(t) + γk(t) zk(t),    γk(t) = 0.18 [E(t)/100] (1 − 0.65 Ik(t)) (6)

The diagonal Controllability Gramian over horizon [0, N] is Wc,kk = Σt [Φk(N, t+1) b̃k(t)]2, with condition number κ(Wc) = λmax / λmin.

4. Empirical Results: EXP-001 & EXP-002

In EXP-001 (CORPUS-001, Spanish university conscription debate), Jensen-Shannon divergence DJS ≥ 0.30 bits recovered all four true camera transitions (Windows #0, #1, #18, #26) without false positives. Speaker SPK_01 achieved the highest Narrative Energy (mean 72.90, peak 76.54 at Window #7, −16.96 dBFS, 203.7 WPM) but the lowest Narrative Inertia (0.151) because he advocated abolishing an existing institution. Conversely, SPK_03 spoke 4.4 dB softer (−21.34 dBFS) yet achieved the highest Inertia (0.668) by anchoring military service in statutory law and taxation.

In EXP-002 (Table 1 & Figure 1), projecting all three works into the 5D NSE Metric Space yields non-degenerate Euclidean separation: ||mC1 − mC2||2 = 0.2711, ||mC2 − mC3||2 = 0.5128, and ||mC1 − mC3||2 = 0.6135 (terminal state distance ||xC1(T) − xC3(T)||2 = 1.0045).

5. Counterfactual Control (EXP-003)

To demonstrate constructive systems synthesis, CounterfactualController.cs solves three discrete-time control problems across our corpus:

  1. Exhaustive 6! = 720 Montage Reachability (CORPUS-001): Holding 100% of the physical footage invariant (H(T) = 2.7574 bits), editorial ordering alone spans sign-reversing intervals across every belief dimension: x1(T) ∈ [−0.2603, +0.5130] (Δ = 0.7733) and x2(T) ∈ [−0.1795, +0.5830] (Δ = 0.7625). Solving minπ ||x(T)||2 yields the Civic Equilibrium Montage π*eq (Table 2), reducing terminal normative bias by −62.0% (||x*(T)||2 = 0.3274 vs. 0.8620) without cutting a single frame.
  2. Non-Verbal Shock Impulse Leverage (CORPUS-002): Attenuating the nuclear roar (−13.68 dBFS → −25 dBFS) and atomic flash cut (DJS = 0.5766 → 0.04 bits) in Windows #4–#8 of LBJ's “Daisy” spot while keeping the transcript identical reduces terminal mobilization x2(T) from +0.2624 to +0.1841—proving that 29.84% of the commercial's state displacement is driven purely by the non-verbal audiovisual shock wave.
  3. Institutional Inertial Shielding (CORPUS-003): Removing presidential institutional anchoring reduces terminal inertia by −0.4657 (0.8921 → 0.4264), whereas accumulated inertia shields the address from sign reversal (x2(T) = +0.2321 > 0) even under a late 30 s high-energy crisis critique perturbation.

6. Conclusion

By unifying physical signal operators (PCM prosody, Jensen-Shannon visual divergence, Shannon stance entropy) with LTV state-space control and exhaustive montage optimization, Narrative Systems Engineering transitions media analysis from qualitative metaphor to a falsifiable, reproducible engineering discipline.
