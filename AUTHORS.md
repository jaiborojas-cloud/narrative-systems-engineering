## Scope of Original Authorship

**Jair Ivan Rojas Ruiz** is the sole originator, conceptual author, and principal investigator of this project, including:

1. **The Original Multimodal Audiovisual Inquiry:** The foundational vision of integrating linguistic, semantic, prosodic/acoustic, visual, chromatic, environmental, sartorial, and material-recurrence observations to quantify and interpret audiovisual works ([`docs/ORIGINAL_IDEA.md`](docs/ORIGINAL_IDEA.md)).
2. **The Three Foundational Frameworks:**
   - *Human Narrative Atlas (HNA)* (`sources/foundational/fundationla-doc-1.pdf`)
   - *Narrative Dynamics Engine (NDE)* (`sources/foundational/fundationla-doc-2.pdf`)
   - *Narrative Systems Engineering (NSE)* (`sources/foundational/fundationla-doc-3.pdf`)
3. **The Core Proposed Narrative Metrics:**
   - **Narrative DNA (`ADN Narrativo`)**
   - **Narrative Energy (`Energía Narrativa`)**
   - **Narrative Inertia (`Inercia Narrativa`)**
   - **Narrative Entropy (`Entropía Narrativa`)**
   - **Narrative Complexity Index (`Índice de Complejidad Narrativa`)**
   - **Narrative Controllability (`Controlabilidad Narrativa`, $x(t+1) = f(x(t), u(t))$) **
